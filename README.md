# Learn the Ship Playbook with Phoebe

Six 45-minute sessions on building a data and AI roadmap that survives contact with a business.
This is the **shared spine** the vertical roadmaps on the same shelf import, so it deliberately
picks no industry: the four moves are the same everywhere and only the answers change. No code
required in any session.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ship-playbook-with-phoebe/

| # | Session | Signature thing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The five categories | See it, explain it, predict it, decide it, do it - a dependency, not a maturity model |
| 2 | The four-way verdict | Automate, augment, build, keep human - a separate axis, passed on a task and never a job |
| 3 | The verdict bench | Twelve candidates through two gates, every number computed |
| 4 | The data gate | Held partly is the dangerous state, and the unlock table nobody expects |
| 5 | The revenue model decides | The same case is worth different amounts, and three famous statistics that do not survive |
| 6 | The roadmap on one page | The artefact a vertical imports, including what is deliberately off it |

The bench in session 3 (`assets/sp-live.js`) holds twelve candidate use cases at an invented
22-site gym chain, against twelve data entities of which six are held in full, three only partly
and three not at all. Every number is computed in the browser from the entity-availability map:
a real gate check per case, a real weighted sum, and a real unlock pass across all twelve
candidates.

What it finds. **Dropping everything that does not clear the gate today takes the plan from 64
weeks to 27 with the revenue fit unchanged at 12**, because the same six cases clear either way
and the rest were only ever consuming the estimate. The three most-requested new-data cases
produce a revenue fit of **0** over 25 weeks. And the single most-requested item on the whole
list needs no data at all, clears every gate in **4 weeks**, and carries a revenue fit of
**zero** - cheap, popular, clean and empty.

The unlock table is the distinctive part. A **cancellation reason field the front desk already
fills in about a third of the time** releases more value than any brand-new data source on the
board: the best of those releases less and would require a standing job forever, while finishing
the half-filled field is a form change.

The chain is invented and labelled as such on every page that quotes a figure, and every effort
number is stipulated rather than estimated. One comparison on the bench reverses if one of those
stipulated numbers changes, and session 3 names which comparison and declines to teach it.

Two of the four moves rest on work read at source: a paper that scored 18,156 tasks across 964
occupations and found few if any occupations where every task qualified, and an essay quoted
directly on why the field carries excess incentives toward automation over augmentation. **The
sequencing rule has no published source at all** - a deliberate search found none - so session 5
presents it as this playbook's own heuristic and says so, rather than borrowing a citation that
does not support it. Session 5 also takes apart the three AI-failure-rate statistics commonly
used to justify roadmaps: one expired by its own terms in 2022, one traces to an unattributed
remark on a conference panel, and one is called "misguided" by a study that went and read its
sources.

Every fact, its verification tier, the tuning decision recorded rather than hidden, and the
claims deliberately left unstated are in `materials/official-course-map.md`.
